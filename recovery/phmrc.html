  • Cause top-1 = fraction whose highest-probability cause is correct. Balanced = mean recall over causes present in hidden records.

  • λ error = mean absolute deviation from empirical donor proportions over supported source–cause cells.

Leakage and validation
  • Historical whole-site source fits used every proposed donor in training. They are ineligible for reuse. New experts use only the saved retained-training partition.
  • The input table is pinned by SHA256; original row position identifies records within that exact file. Every prepared expert must match the training IDs, feature order and cause mapping.
  • All six Gibbs conditions matched exact enumeration on a small test, with maximum checked mean/probability discrepancy below 0.003. The analytic known-Y,H reference and all-Y π-invariance were verified.
  • Source quality remains a separate gate: previous full-site K10 fits had convergence problems. K5 does not guarantee convergence.
How to read the eventual results
  • Arm A is primarily a counts-and-prior check: λ | Y,H ~ Dirichlet(1+n) on supported sources. A prior gives nonzero mass to supported sources with zero realized donors; that is not an implementation error.
  • Fixed-H arms have perfect origin assignment by construction. Their origin accuracy is not a discovery result.
  • When H is fixed and Y hidden, source cause-support restrictions provide legitimate extra oracle information.
  • Origin recovery will be compared with marginal donor-origin and explicitly oracle cause-conditioned donor baselines. Fixed π is not reported as estimated prevalence success.
  • Similar source experts or source-model mismatch can prevent recovery of donor origin/weights even with correct inference.
