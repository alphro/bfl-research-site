Definitions and limitations

  • These are hard classifications from marginal posterior mean probabilities, not sampled assignments or λ weights.
  • Cause and donor predictions are paired record by record. Donor correctness means recovering the actual donor site.
  • A/B receive every cause label; A/C/D receive donor identities. Their perfect supplied-label scores are not predictive recovery.
  • All-record cause accuracy includes 313 supplied labels in C–F. Use the hidden-label cohort for cause prediction.
  • Frozen LCVA source fits have convergence concerns. This is a diagnostic of the fitted experiment, not a validated claim of generalization.

For a source flow m → r, the disease error rate is n(true disease c, true donor m, predicted donor r) / n(true disease c, true donor m). “Cause still correct” uses only records in that flow and disease.
