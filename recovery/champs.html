1. Does knowing the donor improve cause prediction?

Arms D and F both reach 65.34% hidden-cause accuracy (164/251). Their balanced accuracies differ, so equal overall accuracy does not imply identical predictions. D has parameter CSMF 89.24%; F has 85.82%. F recovers donor identity for 61.15% (192/314).
